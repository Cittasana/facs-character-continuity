# Privacy

FACS Character Continuity is an instructions-only Agent Skill. It consists of two Markdown files and contains no code, scripts, MCP servers, hooks, or network calls.

- **No data collection.** The skill collects, stores, and transmits nothing. Cittasana S.R.L. receives no data from anyone who installs or uses it.
- **Your content stays where you put it.** Text and images you share while using the skill remain in your Claude conversation, governed by Anthropic's terms, and in whichever image or video generator you choose to use, governed by that provider's terms.
- **No tracking.** The skill contains no analytics, telemetry, or tracking links.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/Cittasana/facs-character-continuity/issues>.
